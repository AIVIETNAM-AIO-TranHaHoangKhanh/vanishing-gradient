- Suggested humorous caption: “Gradient đi qua từng lớp: đồng đội thì ướt, lớp đầu vẫn khát.”
- Mapping: water represents gradient magnitude; successive players represent layers traversed during backpropagation; the final receiver represents an early network layer.
